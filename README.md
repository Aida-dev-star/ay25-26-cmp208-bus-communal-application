# ay25-26-cmp208-bus-communal-application

Project Title: Easy Bus Communal Application


Problem Statement: The Problem of waiting for Communal buses in the hot sun has impacted every person that
has visited this school. Whether it be regulars or visitors this results in time being wasted and
people missing there personal appointments or plans.


Project Overview: The Easy Bus Communal Application seeks to aid students in getting a bus on campus easily
and immediately when they want.This Application will provide notifications on when there is
a bus on campus available for boarding. It will also provide different services such as
estimated time of arrival for the next bus and time periods throughout the day in which there
is more student waiting for buses it will also give time periods through the day when there is
less people waiting.This application seeks to provide students an easy and stress free wait to
commute back to town from a productive and tiring day of classes practicals.It addresses the
problem of long waiting times for students staff and guests,as there is a lack of suitable bus
waiting areas.

Project Scope:
• Research on and off times for when most commuters wait for bus and vice versa.

• Develop a system that gives accurate information and estimations of bus arrival time.

• Develop a bus stop heatmap where most people wait for buses and where the least
people wait for buses.

• Create a Request Bus feature where if five or more commmuters request a bus a
notification will be sent to the drivers device to notify them of commuters on
campus.Commuters will also be sent a unique identifier for their requested bus e.g
MiniBuses Number Plate.

• Develop an Account Creation system where commuters and drivers will be able to
view the heatmap ,commuters will be able to send requests to drivers and drivers will
be notified of those requests and what location it came from.

• Develop an offline mode that will give estimated bus arrival times based on past data
